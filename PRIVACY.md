# Better EduRec privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Better EduRec is an unofficial Chrome extension that restyles NUS EduRec in your own browser. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National University of Singapore.

## Data the extension handles

- **Academic information** shown on EduRec pages you open: course codes and titles, grades, GPA, academic standing, degree audit units, class timetable, rooms and class groups.
- **Settings**: theme, sidebar state and similar preferences.

## How it is used and stored

- Used only to show the Overview, summaries and timetable to you.
- Stored with `chrome.storage.local` on your device. It is not synced, uploaded, sold, shared, or used for advertising, analytics or profiling.
- The developer never receives any of your data.

## Network requests

- Only when you click **Open in NUSMods**: your course codes are sent to the public NUSMods API (`api.nusmods.com`), and a nusmods.com link containing your course codes and class groups opens in a new tab. NUSMods' own privacy policy applies there.
- No other requests are made. The extension loads no remote code.

## Your control

- Delete saved data at any time with **Clear summaries** in the toolbar popup, or by removing the extension.
